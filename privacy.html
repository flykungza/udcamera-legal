Privacy Policy

UDCAMERA Content Publisher  ·  Effective September 9, 2026

This policy explains what data UDCAMERA Content Publisher accesses through TikTok's, Facebook's, and YouTube's APIs, and how that data is used. The tool connects only to social media accounts owned by UDCAMERA itself — it does not interact with, collect from, or publish to any other person's account.

01

Data accessed via TikTok

When a UDCAMERA-owned TikTok account is connected, the tool requests only the scopes needed to operate:

ScopeUsed for
user.info.basicConfirming which UDCAMERA account is connected
video.publishUploading and publishing video files to that same account

The tool does not read TikTok followers, comments, direct messages, analytics, or any other user's data.

02

Content supplied by UDCAMERA staff

Video files, cover images, titles, descriptions, and hashtags are entered directly by UDCAMERA staff for each post. This is UDCAMERA's own marketing material, sent to TikTok's, Facebook's, or YouTube's servers only to publish it — it is not stored on any other server, and is not sold, shared, or reused for any purpose beyond publishing the post the staff member requested.

03

Access tokens

OAuth access tokens issued by TikTok, Facebook, and YouTube are stored locally on the device running the tool and are used only to authorize posting to UDCAMERA's own connected accounts. They are never transmitted to any third party.

04

Retention & revocation

Because the tool holds no data belonging to the public — only its own operator's connection tokens — there is no third-party user data to retain or delete. UDCAMERA staff can revoke the tool's access at any time from TikTok's (or Facebook's/YouTube's) app-permissions settings, which immediately invalidates all stored tokens.
